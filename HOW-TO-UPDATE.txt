HOW TO UPDATE THE LIVE SITE

1. Go to yoursite.com/?edit and enter your passphrase.
2. Change whatever you want. It saves as a draft in your browser only.
3. Edit panel > Publish > Export settings.json > Copy.
4. On github.com, open your repo > settings.json > pencil icon (Edit).
5. Select all, paste, click "Commit changes".
6. Vercel redeploys on its own. Live in about a minute.

New clips: upload them to the media folder on GitHub first
(Add file > Upload files), then point the project at them in edit mode.

Default passphrase: paddington  (change it in the edit panel, then publish)
